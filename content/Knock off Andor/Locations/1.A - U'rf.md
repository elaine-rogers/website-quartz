Current:

Home planet of [[Bai]] in the [[D'ur'ik]] system. 

Current:

It is nestled in the valley of two folds in the veil, connecting two arms on either side of the galaxy. Because of this it is a massive trading hub, the entire planet has been transformed from a quiet mountain forests to a bustling metropolis. 

U'ft is currently ruled by the [[Cái]] dynasty who rule the land through money both above and below the table. Originating from the [[Iacchus Guild]], the family was placed their as a pawn to control the region and keep the region firmly in the Guild's control. Over the next century however the family would see itself as its own entity separate from the Guild, causing a war of subterfuge as both attempt to maintain control in the region. 

Due to the ability to move millions of light years in a matter of days, the planet is a thriving center for run away criminals and those who chase them. 

History:

Terraformed by humans during the initial space age, the planet was turned into a thriving taiga. It is not known what the planet was used for or why it was abandoned, but in humans absence the Japanese Raccoon Dog, or [[Tanuki]], evolved into the dominate species. Because of their semi-solitary nature cities would only become commonplace once the fold in the veil was discovered by the Iacchus Guild. 

As there were not nations on the planet, colonization was swift and brutal. In the matter of a century the land was turned from a cold rain forest to a concrete jungle of neon. The population would explode as people flooded the planet.
