#god 

The Secundus generation of gods formed from the body of [[Primus]]. Very basic concepts and without a will of their own, simply effecting their environment like a force of nature. 

Once [[Humans|Humans]] begin to discover and worship these beings they start to become more specific and gain a consciousness of their own. The more worshippers the more intelligence and power they possess. This gives rise to the [[Tertius]] generation.
