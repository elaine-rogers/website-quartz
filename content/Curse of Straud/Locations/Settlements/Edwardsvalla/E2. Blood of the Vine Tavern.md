Owned by a couple, [[Katya Stamatin|Katya]] and Andre Stamatin. They have 3 children- Anna 16, Farkhad 6, Simon 2. The only person with a soul in the family is Katya. 

The tavern has been passed down for generations through the Stamatin family line. Its dingy but warm and has several rooms upstairs for travelers to rent.
