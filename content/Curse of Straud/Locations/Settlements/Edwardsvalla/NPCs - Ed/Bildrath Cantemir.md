#NPC
LE 

Background:
Owner of the [[E1. Bildrath's Mercantile |Bildrath's Mercantile]] and brother to [[Parpol Cantemir]]

He has a Neapolitan complex, exacerbated by the presence of his brother who he resents for being his parents' favorite. He is the only shop in town forcing everyone in it to buy from him or buy from the occasional caravans of [[Vistani]]. Because of this he hate them for stealing his business and will try to convince the party to hate them as well. 

Goals: 
Get as much money as possible and get rid of his brother. 

Notes for the Party:
If pressed he will call on his brother for backup. He will openly berate his brother and insult him when he does this. He will likely try to flirt with Lilith.
