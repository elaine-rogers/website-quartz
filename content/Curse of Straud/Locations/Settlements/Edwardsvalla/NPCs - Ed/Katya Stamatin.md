#NPC
CN

Background:
Born in Edwardsvalla. She is in charge of the [[E2. Blood of the Vine Tavern|Blood of the Vine Tavern]] which she inherited from her late father and mother. She married her Husband Andre at a young age to avoid scandal after becoming pregnant. Her husband is a Husk as well as her three children- Anna 16, Farkhad 6, Simon 2. 

Her father owed a loan to a group of Vistani that frequent the bar regularly. They work for Straud and regularly report back to him on the goings on.

She is very aware that her husband and children are not like her and resents them for this. If the party stays the night she alludes to this fact and will have a mental breakdown where she will threaten Simon with a kitchen knife and attempt to make him cry. If approached she reacts with shame at her actions and will explain that her no one in her family has a soul. She will say that she wants a fresh start away from children that don't cry and talk back and away from a husband who never laughs.  


Goals: 
Keep her business running. 

She longs for an escape from her life.


Notes for the Campaign: 
She is aware that Lilith is a vampire from the pin that she was given by her.
