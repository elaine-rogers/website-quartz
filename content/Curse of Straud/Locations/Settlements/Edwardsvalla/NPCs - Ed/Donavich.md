#NPC 
LG

The clergy man responsible for [[Edwardsvalla]]'s [[E5. Church|Church]]. His son [[Doru]] died the previous year trying to over throw Straud however came back as a vampire. Donavich locked his son in the basement and has continuously prayed for the last year to save him. 

If the players kill his son he becomes catatonic and will not be able to help the party.
