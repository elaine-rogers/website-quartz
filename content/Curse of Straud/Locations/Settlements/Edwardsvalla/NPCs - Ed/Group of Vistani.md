#NPC 

Background:
The group of [[Vistani]] are composed of 6 people, 3 males, 3 females. They act as the towns bank. They are known for regularly sitting in at places that owe them money and are not above violence to get their money. Despite this their interest rates are fair and they are willing to negotiate different payment methods- specifically secrets and favors. They report what they find back to [[Strahd Von Zarovich|Straud]] directly. 

Goals: Expand their venture into the other towns to get as much money and information as possible. They are mainly stopped by the local governments not allowing banks to operate within their city limits. 

Notes for Campaign:
Names of female Vistani is Alenka, Mirabel, and Sorvia
Names of male Vistani is Jezariah, Mongo, Anslo
