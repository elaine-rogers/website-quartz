
Local gathering place for the Vallakians. It is owned by [[Urwin Martikov]], who considers it a safe place from evils. All doors and windows can be barred from the inside in case of emergency.

Staying for the night costs half a gold piece per person, with breakfast included (Beet soup and bread). Wolf steak costs half a gold piece as well. Pint of Purple Grapemash No.3 win costs 3cp, a pint of Red Dragon Crush costs 1sp. Urwin and his family makes the wines and will be hurt if the party complains about them. 

The inn's supply of wine is almost depleted because the last delivery from the Wizard of Wines winery is overdue. If the players say they are adventures Urwin will ask them to find out what happened to the wine, promising free room and board if they return with the wine.  

Urwin and his wife, [[Danika Dorakava]], are part of a secret society called the [[Keepers of the Last Light]] which oppose Straud. They have two sons, Ram and Rom. They are 11 and 9. The [[Hauss Kindness]] are close friends with the inn keepers, with [[Fróðin Hauss]] working as their barmaid. The Keepers of the Last Light will watch over the party when they can. If they get into trouble a group of 1d4 people will come help them in Vallaki. 

Danika usually tends the bar while Urwin is in the kitchen, their children playing underfoot. Most patrons take kindly to the children and act as friends to them. 

Between dawn and noon there are no patrons there, except for those who stayed the night. From noon to dusk there are 2d4 Vallakians there, from dusk to midnight there are 2d8. 

Wolf Hunters might be present, [[Szoldar Szostarovich]] and [[Yevgeni Krushkin]] are local hunters that frequent the Inn. They kill wolves and sell their meat for a living. Both men are grim and haunted. They are always looking for coin, and have much information about the lay of the lands of Barovia. They are willing to serve as guides for 5gp per day, or provide directions to landmarks for a free drink. They refuse to travel at night unless the party pays 100gp. They talk briskly, but have good humor. 

The Wachter  Brothers, [[Nikolai Wachter||Nikolai]] and [[Karl Wachter ]]are brothers from noble birth. They drunkards always looking for trouble. Their mother is [[Lady Fiona Wachter]] but they never talk about her. They couldn't care less about the local politics. 

Rictavio, also known as [[Rudolph Van Richten]], is posing as a bard. He claims to be part of a traveling circus and regales various neigh impossible stories about his adventures. He has been staying at the entire time the party has been adventuring. When he arrived he picked up a monkey called Piccolo, but gave it to the toy maker since the inn wouldn't allow it inside. While at the inn he's been gathering information on the Keepers of the Last Light and wereravens as well as the Vistani, especially the ones that live just outside of town ([[n.9]])
