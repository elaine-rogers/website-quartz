#NPC
A mediocre carpenter and a troubled man. No one wants to be around him because of his work. 

Last month, he was visited by Straud who was disguised as a nobleman who promised good business in exchange for a favor. Since then many vampire spawn have made his workshop their den. The vampires plan on attacking [[St. Andral's Church]]. 

When he learned about the bones the vampire spawn ordered him to take them, which he paid [[Milivoj]] to do. 

Every window of his shop is frosted glass with a lattice of ironworks locked from the inside. The outside doors of the shop are barred from the inside. If the player knocks he shouts, "CLOSED, GO AWAY", without opening the door. If the players accuse him of stealing the bones he repeats for them to leave. 

If the players break into the shop Henrik will tell them where to find the bones. 

The players can report the theft to the [[Baron Vargas Vallakovich]] he will send 4 guards and arrest Henrik. If they come during the day then he will lead them to the bones, otherwise he will tell them where they are but refuse to get them himself for fear of the vampires.
