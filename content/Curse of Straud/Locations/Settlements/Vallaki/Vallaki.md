Town in [[Barovia]]

Notes for Campaign: 
They approach from the East (right) of the map 

Ruled by [[Baron Vargas Vallakovich]]

The town is in a perpetual state of festivals and preparing for the next one. The last festival the heads of wolves were paraded in the streets on pikes. The next event will be the [[Festival of the Blazing Sun]]. 

A 15 foot wall surrounds the town, made of vertical logs sharpened to points. There is scaffolding to allow the guards to look over the wall. 

There are 3 gates that go into the town. The north gate- also known as Zarovich gate, the west gate- also known as the Sunset gate, and the east gate- also known as the Morning gate or Mourning gate. The gates are locked with heavy chains and padlocks at night, but during the day the gates are unlocked, though they are kept closed. Two guards are inside each gate, carrying pikes (10ft reach, 1d10 + 1 piercing damage). They can stab through the bars of the gates. They are suspicious of anyone from outside and need a DC 20 diplomacy check to be allowed in at night. There are 24 guards total in the town and a militia of townsfolk 50 strong if need be.

Common Knowledge:
- [[Blue Water Inn]] offers food, wine, and shelter. A stranger with pointed ears is staying there, coming from a distant land in a carnival wagon.
- The burgomaster, Baron Vargas Vallakovich, declared that the festival will be held in the town square in 3 days. The last festival was the Wolf's head jamboree.
- There has been a festival every week for almost a decade. Some believe they keep [[Strahd Von Zarovich]] at bay. Others consider them annoyances
- Those who talk badly about the festivals are thrown into the [[Stocks||stocks]] for being in league with Strahd or taken directly to the burgomaster's mansion
- The burgomaster's henchman, [[Izek Strazni]], has a history of violence and has a deformity on his arm that allows him to conjure fire. He keeps the burgomaster's enemies at bay.
- Lady Fiona hates the burgomaster, who is quoted saying "I'd rather serve the devil than a madman". She owns an [[area N4||old house]] in town and rarely leaves her estate. She has two sons, [[Nikolai Wachter||Nikolai]] and [[Karl Wachter||Karl]], who are local troublemakers. She also has a mad daughter named [[Stella Wachter||Stella]]. The burgomaster doesn't bother them because he is afraid of Fiona because of her family's connections to Straud. 
- Purple Flashes of light have been seen from the attic of the burgomaster's mansion.
- Wolves and dire wolves prowl the woods at night and attack people on Old Svalich Road. Hunters have managed to kill some of them but more keep coming. 
- Its too dangerous to go fishing at [[Lake Zarovich]] because of the wolves but [[Bluto Krogarov]], the town drunk, keeps trying. He sets out every morning and returns every evening, he hasn't caught any fish in months. 
- No one has seen the [[Mad Mage of Mount Baratok]] in quiet some time. Folks used to see him by Lake Zarovich, shooting bolts of lightning into the water to kill fish. The locals recommend using fishing boats on the south shore to cross the lake to meet him. 
- There is a Vistani Camp southwest of the town, they are not friendly. Vistani are not allowed inside of town. 
- West of town there is a haunted mansion. Legends say there was a dragon that died there long ago 
- South of town there is a village that has been abandoned there for decades. Its burgomaster committed an offence that incurred the wrath of Strahd.
