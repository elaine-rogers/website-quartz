The bones of St. Andral has protected the church from Straud, which were buried beneath the church in a crypt. Only Father Lucian knew about the bones until he told Yeska about them. Soon after they were stolen. Yeska told one other person about the bones but would not tell Father Lucian who it was. 

The culprit is Milivoj, whom Father Lucian suspects, but is reluctant to confront him due to the boys temperamental nature. Father has not reported the theft in fear of the distress it would cause. He will tell Artz Ludwig about the theft in hopes that the party can help.

To access the crypt, Milivoj must pry open the chapel floorboards with his shovel. DC 10 charisma check he will admit that Yeska told him about the bones. He will also admit to passing the information along to [[Henrik van der Voort]]

The missing bones has left the church vulnerable to attacks by Straud. If the bones are returned, the church is once again protected by the hallow spell.
