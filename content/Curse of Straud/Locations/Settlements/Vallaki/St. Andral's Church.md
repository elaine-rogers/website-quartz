Church is detected to the Morning Lord.

Named after [[St. Andral]] who is buried on the premises.

Father [[Lucian Petrovich]] oversees the church with help of an orphan and alter boy, [[Yeska]]. A boy named [[Milivoj]] tended the grounds. 

At night the church is packed with 2d6 + 6 Vallakian adults and 2d6 children. Father Lucian offers prayers and the protection of the Morning Lord and St. Andral's. Among the people is an old woman named [[Willemina Rikalova]]. Her son, [[Udo Lukovich]], was imprisoned by the burgomaster for speaking out. She prays for her son's freedom. 

Once the bones are returned the site becomes hallowed ground.
