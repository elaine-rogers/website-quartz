LG

She is incredibly cautious of the players but if they are brought by her brother,[[Ismark Kolyana]], she will trust them implicitly. She will refuse to leave until her adopted father, [[Ismark the Greater]], is brought to a proper burial at the [[E5. Church|Church]]. 

She is strong willed and will do everything she can to help the mission to take her to [[Vallaki]]. She is not aware that she is adopted and has little memories of her early childhood. 

She is loved by [[Strahd Von Zarovich]] because of her resemblance to [[Tatyana]], Straud's former love.
