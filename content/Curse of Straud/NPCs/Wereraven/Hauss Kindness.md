The Hauss kindness is 9 strong, 7 adults and 2 children. They live in the [[Hallus]], one the outskirts of town. They are regarded as a strange group who mostly keep to themselves, but are generous and kind. 

They migrated as a group from [[Krezk]] before the children were born. They are an off shoot of the larger Kindness there. They were sent with the main goal of having a secondary Kindness in a different town and for a larger information network.
