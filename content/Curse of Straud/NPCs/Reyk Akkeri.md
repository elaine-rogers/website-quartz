#NPC 

Currently in [[Krezk]]. The father of [[Ravina Droph]]. He is half Vistani, which allows him to travel outside of [[Barovia]], where he met [[Agatha Droph]]. 

History:

He started off as a precocious young boy, trickster like in manner. He was known as the family clown, constantly cracking jokes and the like. He would visit the outside occasionally with his mother.

He had gotten hit with BPD later in life, causing him to spiral into depression. He fell in love with Agatha Droph soon after they met and has never recovered from her absence. 

"She is a whirlwind of a woman, like a bull in a china shop but the shop is your life, but despite that you can't help but be thankful she destroyed it all- you always wanted to break it all but never had the guts, but she did. I will never forget it, she took a cigarette out of my mouth, just pop, out of my mouth, took a long drag and then told me my taste in tobacco was shit. That was when I fell in love with her."
