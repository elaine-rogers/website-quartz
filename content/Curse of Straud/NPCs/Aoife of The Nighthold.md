Wife of [[Rudolph Van Richten]]
Usurper of the [[Nighthold]].

A young princess [[fey]] whose kingdom was lost to the Nighthold. Her entire court was massacred by [Eldest](https://pathfinderwiki.com/wiki/Eldest "Eldest") [Count Ranalc](https://pathfinderwiki.com/wiki/Count_Ranalc "Count Ranalc") and his men. Vowing for revenge, Aoife went into hiding. Eventually, she came upon one of Count Ranalc's horses who had lost its wings and been abandoned. The horse and fey made a deal, to sever her wings and sew it into the horses back, in exchange they would get their revenge. Count Ranalc would be killed by their hands leaving a power vacuum, one in which Aoife intends to fill. 

During one of Aoife's campaigns, she would happen across a lost Rudolph and lead him back to his plane of existence. During their journey they would fall in love, Rudolph vowing help her take her realm when he became strong enough. In return, Aoife would give [[Betsy]] to Rudolph to protect him.
