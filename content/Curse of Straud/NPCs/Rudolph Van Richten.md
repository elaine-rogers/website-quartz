#NPC
Human Vampire, Artificer

Background:
Grew up training to become a knight for his town. At a young age a necromancer had their creations overwhelmed the town, since then he made a vow to kill as many of these monster creators as possible. 

While traveling through the fey wilds to kill one such enemy he ended up saving an Arch Fey- [[Aoife of The Nighthold|Aoife]] and becoming her husband at the age of 24. 

As he traveled the lands and slipped between planes he garnered a formattable reputation, one he used to his advantage. When he journeyed to [[Barovia|Straud's land]] he thought it would be a simple job, this overconfidence would be his undoing.

He would fight [[Strahd Von Zarovich|Straud]] and be bitten but not killed, thus turning him into a vampire. Although Straud had one the fight he was gravely injured and feared what Richten could do to him should he gather allies. As a result he refused to let Richten leave and sends his vampire thrall to watch and attack him. Richten spent centuries trying to get into Straud's castle or catch him off guard. 

Goals:
To defeat Straud and to find a way to cure vampirism
He will jeopardize the mission for those he cares about- [[Esmerelda Radanavich|Esmerelda]] 


Notes for Campaign:
He has a horse named Betsy that he got from his wife. Betsy is fey but can look like a normal horse and disguises itself as such. 

When Lilith left he ran into a traveling circus on their way to [[Vallaki]]. He struck a deal with them, in exchange for protection he would act as their ring master and hide amongst the crowd of performers. This allows him to go into the city without suspicion and begin to collect intel on the various settlements.
