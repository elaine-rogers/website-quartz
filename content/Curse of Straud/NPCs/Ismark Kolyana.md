#NPC 
LG

Also known as Ismark the Lesser. He is a kind man who will invite the players to join him for a drink at the [[E2. Blood of the Vine Tavern|Blood of the Vine Tavern]]. He will tell the players about his sister and asks to hire them to help escort them to [[Vallaki]]. He will tell the players that his sister is desired by [[Strahd Von Zarovich]] for reasons unknown to him or anyone else. He will pay as much as he is able (probably around 250 gold) though can be convinced to give more by taking out a loan that he will have to later pay back.
