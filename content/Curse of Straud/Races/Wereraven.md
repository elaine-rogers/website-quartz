#race 

Secretive and cautious race, they trust one another but have a hard time trusting outsiders. 

Light weapons, avoid biting to not spread the curse. 

A kindness of Wereravens is a group of ravens from 7-12 in number. They get along with ravens and hide among them. 

They collect buts and baubles and love sharing what they find. They hide their magical items to prevent them from falling into the wrong hands.
